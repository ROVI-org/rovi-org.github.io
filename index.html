The Rapid Operational Validation Initiative (ROVI) by the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) aims to accelerate commercial deployments and reduce the time from lab-to-market for emerging energy storage technologies. [1, 2]

OBJECTIVE: Develop new accelerated testing methodologies, AI/ML based analysis tools & validation methods to predict 15+ years of investment-grade performance with < 1-year of data.

Our mission

Greatly reduce the time required for emerging energy storage technologies to go from lab to market.

DOE's Rapid Operational Validation Initiative addresses critical gaps in the data needed to evaluate the long-term performance of energy storage technologies, and accelerates the pace of technology development.

Many innovative storage technologies are promising candidates to meet cost and performance targets such as DOE's Long Duration Storage Shot, and to reach the widespread deployment a reliable, clean and affordable grid will need. ROVI develops tools that accelerate the testing and validation each of them must pass to succeed commercially.

Using artificial intelligence and machine learning on large sets of storage performance data at different scales, ROVI generates lifetime performance predictions for new technologies with minimal real-time testing. Models are validated with data from field systems supported by DOE's Office of Electricity (OE) and Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations (OCED).

DOE program page

How ROVI shortens validation

1

Test and simulate

Laboratory cell testing and model simulations across chemistries produce consistent, well-described data sets.

2

Learn and predict

Adaptive AI/ML models forecast degradation and operational lifetime from early-life data.

3

Validate in the field

Predictions are checked against field-deployed systems supported by DOE OE and OCED demonstrations.

DataHub development

One secure home for energy storage data

The ROVI team built a flexible, secure data architecture that ingests complex data streams and supplies consolidated data sets for project evaluations and advanced machine learning.

  • Machine learning models

    Predict battery degradation and performance across operating conditions and environmental factors.

  • Data validation framework

    Protocols that keep data quality and consistency across laboratory and field deployment sites.

  • Real-time analytics

    Live monitoring and analysis for continuous assessment of system performance and health.

  • Collaborative research

    Shared data access and coordinated research across DOE facilities.
