DOE's Rapid Operational Validation Initiative (ROVI) is intended to address critical gaps in data needs to evaluate the long-term performance of energy storage technologies and to accelerate the pace for technology development. The six lab ROVI team (ANL-INL-NLR-ORNL-PNNL-Sandia) is primarily focused on accelerating the time from lab to market for long duration energy storage (LDES) systems by employing data-driven tools to predict their operational lifetimes. ROVI will develop a suite of adaptive AI/ML modeling tools based on data from laboratory testing and model simulations that will be validated with data from field deployed systems supported by DOE's Office of Electricity (OE) and Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations (OCED).

Mission

Predict 15+ years of investment-grade storage performance from less than one year of data.

The Office of Electricity’s Rapid Operational Validation Initiative (ROVI) exists to shorten the path from laboratory energy-storage technologies to commercial deployment. ROVI builds data standards, accelerated testing methods, and AI/ML tools that turn limited laboratory, field, and simulated performance data into lifetime forecasts for long-duration storage — validated against DOE Office of Electricity and Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations deployments.
